FORMULA

ΔSmix = −R Σ[nᵢ ln(xᵢ)]; xᵢ = nᵢ/Σnᵢ; lim(nᵢ→0⁺) nᵢ ln(nᵢ/Σnⱼ) = 0

Assumptions

Each row represents one distinct chemical species. Combine amounts of the same gas into one row. Required: equal initial temperature and pressure for all gases. Excluded: reaction, phase change and nonideal behavior. Zero-amount rows are ignored and at least one row must have positive amount. With only one gas species the mixing entropy is zero.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
